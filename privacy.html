Privacy notice: Performance Readiness Assessment

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

Cirquenastics Ltd runs this assessment. Contact: info@cirquenastics.com.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address
  • Your Instagram handle, if you choose to give it
  • Your answers to the assessment and the scores calculated from them
  • Your email preference (Yes or No) and whether you joined the Cirquenastics Club waitlist

We do not ask for medical details. One question asks whether pain or injury has interrupted your training; it is used only to show you a safety note.

How we use it

  • To show you your result and understand your training priorities if you contact us
  • To send you training tips and updates by email, only if you chose Yes
  • To email you when the Cirquenastics Club opens, only if you joined the waitlist

We do not sell your data. Our legal bases are your consent (marketing and waitlist emails) and our legitimate interest in providing the assessment you requested.

Who processes it for us

Your submission is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as your IP address. If you opted in to emails, your name and email are added to our email platform.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing info@cirquenastics.com. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
